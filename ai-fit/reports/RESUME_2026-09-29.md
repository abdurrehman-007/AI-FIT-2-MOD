## Real GPU experiment
Same model/weights and original protected-mask pipeline; no production resolution change. Temporary VAE memory-efficient mode enabled only in experiment. At 384x512 blue and brown passed with 17.78s and 13.47s. At 576x768 blue passed in 31.97s, showing closer blue-grey colour/detail on inspection but remaining original cuff and pattern artifacts. Brown failed with a CUDA RuntimeError allocation failure. Memory contention from a concurrently running live service was discovered during the follow-up 480x640 trial; only the experiment processes were stopped. Thus no reliable higher-resolution acceptance or general speed/VRAM claim is made. The working production 384x512 configuration remains unchanged. No model/mask experiment promoted based on one successful example.
